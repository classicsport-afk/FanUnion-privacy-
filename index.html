Privacy Policy · Last updated: March 24, 2026

Introduction

FanUnion ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data when you use the FanUnion mobile application.

Information We Collect

When you use FanUnion, we may collect the following information:

  • Email address — used to create and manage your account.
  • Team preferences — the NFL teams you follow or support.
  • Voting predictions — your in-game play call predictions and poll responses.
  • Campaign pledges — any fan campaign commitments you make within the app.
  • Device information — limited device and push notification tokens needed to deliver notifications.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to operate and improve the FanUnion experience, including:

  • Providing core app functionality such as polls, play call predictions, and leaderboards.
  • Generating aggregated fan sentiment data and analytics.
  • Sending push notifications about game events, poll results, and app updates.
  • Maintaining the security and integrity of your account.

We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties for their own commercial purposes. Any aggregated or anonymized fan sentiment data we publish does not identify you individually.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal information from our systems within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

Deleting Your Account and Data

You have the right to delete your account and all associated personal data at any time. You can do this in one of two ways:

  • In the app: Go to your Profile, tap the settings menu, and select "Reset FanUnion" to initiate account deletion.
  • By email: Send a deletion request to fanunionapp@gmail.com and we will process it within 30 days.

Push Notifications

FanUnion may send push notifications to keep you informed during live games and for other app activity. You can disable push notifications at any time through your device's notification settings.

Children's Privacy

FanUnion is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of FanUnion after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at:
